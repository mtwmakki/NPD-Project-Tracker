1 · Two layers, never mixed

Every surface in the tracker belongs to one of two layers. The layer decides the material, not how important the component is.

Interactive layer: glass

  • Floating tab bar (phone) and sidebar rail (tablet, laptop)
  • Toolbar buttons: settings, theme, sign out, sync pill
  • Range control in the top bar (Today / This week …)
  • Overlays: settings menu, toasts, date picker, command bar, selection bar
  • Back-to-top button

Content layer: frosted material

  • Dashboard tiles, panels, the overdue banner
  • Project and inquiry lists, task rows, alert rows
  • Project page sections, milestones, board columns
  • Forms and sheets
  • Sign-in card

Thin by default, ultra-thin only over calm backgrounds, solid where text is dense (forms, long notes). Content materials get a soft top edge but never the controls' rim, sheen or glint.

Do
  • Use glass only where something floats over content and moves or collapses.
  • Keep a soft grey light-and-shade backdrop so the frosting has tone to work with.
  • Give every glass shape the full shine (rim, sheen, specular lines).
Don't
  • Put glass on cards, lists or anything people read for long.
  • Stack glass on glass.
  • Tint glass with the accent, except the selected tab and the one primary action.

3 · The shine

A flat 1px border reads as plastic. Real glass catches light unevenly, so every glass shape gets three layers:

  1. Gradient rim: a 1px ring, brightest at the top-left where the light hits, nearly gone along the middle, a softer catch at the bottom-right. Drawn as a masked gradient, so it follows any corner radius.
  2. Top sheen: a wide, soft radial highlight entering from the top edge, blended with soft-light so it lifts the colour underneath instead of painting white over it.
  3. Specular lines and glint: the inner 1px highlight from §1 along the top, a faint one along the bottom, and a small highlight that follows the pointer (or finger) across the surface.

In dark mode every layer drops to about a third of its strength, so controls glint instead of glowing.

5 · Motion

  • Only cheap properties move: transform, opacity and clip-path. Nothing that changes layout (height, padding, margins) is animated, which was the cause of the jerky opening before.
  • Opening uses the spring, closing doesn't: things open with cubic-bezier(.175,.885,.32,1.275) over 400ms (a small overshoot) and close with a quick ease-in in about 250ms, so they never bounce on the way out.
  • Morph from the source: the menu starts as the exact 44 × 44 shape of the settings button and the clip opens out from that corner; its rows fade in 32ms apart once the shape is mostly open.
  • Changing tabs: the selection is one glass drop (the lens) that slides to the new tab on a real spring (about 5% overshoot, 460–640ms depending on distance). While it travels it stretches toward the new tab and squashes slightly, then settles; the new icon dips and bounces as the lens lands. Press and slide along the bar and the lens lifts, follows your finger, the icon under it turns blue as you pass, and it snaps to the nearest tab when you let go. The tablet and laptop sidebar rail uses the same lens, moving up and down.
  • Folding the tab bar: the capsule's width springs from 232 to 64 while the row of tabs slides so the current tab stays centred; the others fade. Tapping the folded pill unfolds it and never navigates.
  • Entrance: content fades in first; then the toolbar, tab bar and search rise 14px into place, 80ms apart.
  • Press: 0.97 while pressed, 1.03 on hover with a mouse, 300ms spring back.
  • Reduced motion: every transition and entrance is instant.

6 · Type and contrast

Typeface: Geist for text and headings, Geist Mono for codes, IDs and big numbers (signed off), unless you pick one of the Trap options; Trap would ship with the tracker as five small font files (10 KB each). Both load from Google Fonts with the system font as fallback, so it looks the same on iPhone, Fold and Windows.

In the tracker, sizes follow the iOS 27 kit: Large Title 34, Title 2 22, Title 3 20, list titles 17, secondary 15, details 13.

8 · Implementation in the tracker

The tracker is plain HTML and CSS, so the §4 component becomes one .glass recipe plus tokens, scoped to the Liquid Glass style so the other ten styles never see it.

:root[data-look="K"]{
  --g-blur: 24px; --g-sat: 180%;
  --fill-bar: rgba(255,255,255,.25);  --fill-btn: rgba(255,255,255,.20);  --fill-over: rgba(255,255,255,.82);
  --rim-a: .95; --rim-m: .10; --rim-b: .55; --sheen: .42; --glint: .55;
  --spring: cubic-bezier(.175,.885,.32,1.275); --t-morph: 400ms; --t-press: 300ms;
}
.glass{
  backdrop-filter: blur(var(--g-blur)) saturate(var(--g-sat));
  box-shadow: 0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.12), inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,.6), inset 0 -1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,.14);
}
.glass::before{ /* gradient rim */
  content:""; position:absolute; inset:0; border-radius:inherit; padding:1px;
  background: linear-gradient(140deg, rgba(255,255,255,var(--rim-a)), rgba(255,255,255,var(--rim-m)) 34% 64%, rgba(255,255,255,var(--rim-b)));
  mask: linear-gradient(#000 0 0) content-box exclude, linear-gradient(#000 0 0);
}
.glass::after{ /* top sheen + pointer glint */
  content:""; position:absolute; inset:0; border-radius:inherit; mix-blend-mode: soft-light;
  background: radial-gradient(130% 90% at 50% -30%, rgba(255,255,255,var(--sheen)), transparent 58%),
              radial-gradient(90px circle at var(--mx) var(--my), rgba(255,255,255,var(--glint)), transparent 70%);
}

In the tracker, .glass is applied through the existing selectors (.ibtn, .sync, .tabbar, .side, #pop, .toast, #dp, .cmd, .selbar, #totop, .top .seg), so no markup changes are needed. One small script sets --mx / --my on whichever glass element the pointer is over.

Changes to pull request #6 after sign-off

  • Tokens switched to the chosen mix (24px blur, .20 buttons, gradient rims).
  • The three-layer shine on every glass element.
  • Menu and overlays grow out of their trigger with clip-path; tab bar folds with width + slide, not by collapsing tabs.
  • Proportions from §4 (44 controls, tab bar h56 / r28, menu rows 40 / r16).
  • Reduced-transparency fallback, reduced motion, tertiary text at .60.

9 · Accessibility and performance

  • Reduce transparency: glass becomes solid #F7F8FA / #26282E and content materials become solid, all with no blur.
  • Reduce motion: no springs, no entrance, no fold, no menu morph.
  • Focus: a 2px system-blue ring outside every glass control.
  • Touch: every glass control at least 44 × 44; icon-only controls keep an accessible name.
  • Glass audit before each release: at most 6 glass surfaces per screen, none on a card, list or form, no glass inside glass.
  • Performance: the glint updates only through CSS variables (no layout), the blur itself is never animated, and glass never sits on scrolling rows.
